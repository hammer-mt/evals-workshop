# Returns and refunds

Snapshot of basecampoutfitters.example/help/returns, captured 2026-10-20.

- You can return unworn, unused items in their original packaging within 30 days of delivery.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of the return arriving at our warehouse.
- Items that have been worn outdoors or used cannot be returned. If a worn item is faulty, see the warranty policy instead.
- Personalized or custom-engraved items are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged unless they arrive damaged.
- A $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund, unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.
- Exchanges for a different size or color are free within 30 days of delivery.
- Customer service staff cannot extend the 30-day window.
