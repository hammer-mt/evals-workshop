# Warranty

Snapshot of basecampoutfitters.example/help/warranty, captured 2026-10-20.

- Footwear and outerwear are covered against manufacturing defects (for example, soles separating or seams failing) for 1 year from delivery.
- Normal wear and tear and damage from misuse are not covered.
- To make a claim, a human agent reviews photos of the defect. The assistant cannot approve a warranty claim itself.
- Approved claims get a repair, a replacement, or store credit. Cash refunds are not offered for warranty claims.
