# Shipping

Snapshot of basecampoutfitters.example/help/shipping, captured 2026-10-20.

- We ship to the 50 US states only.
- Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days and is free on orders over $75. Below $75 it costs $7.95.
- Expedited shipping takes 2 business days and costs $15.
- We do not offer next-day or same-day delivery.
- Orders ship from Denver, Colorado within 1 business day.
- Once an order has shipped, the delivery address cannot be changed. Before it ships, a human agent can change it.
