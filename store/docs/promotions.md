# Promotion terms

Snapshot of basecampoutfitters.example/help/promotion-terms, captured 2026-10-20.

- FALL20: 20% off full-price footwear. Valid October 1–15, 2026. This promotion has ended.
- Basecamp Rewards: members earn 1 point per $1 spent. 100 points can be redeemed for $5 off.
- We do not offer student, military or employer discounts.
- Only one promotion can be applied per order.
- We do not have a price-matching policy.
